# WhatsApp — first touch (step 0)

Use ONLY when product.md documents a compliant WhatsApp business channel and
the ICP market accepts business WhatsApp. Otherwise email.

Structure (<= 60 words, 2-3 short paragraphs, no links, no media):

1. Who I am, in one line: name + company (people don't read anonymous
   WhatsApp).
2. The personalized observation + pain, one line each — same specificity bar
   as email.
3. Soft ask: "Worth a short call this week?" / "Can I send a 2-min overview?"

Rules:
- No link in message 1 (spam-filter + trust). Send materials only after a
  reply.
- Time-of-day: business hours in the LEAD's timezone; the follow-up engine
  respects the same rule.
- One message. No double-texting before a reply.
- If they reply "stop"/"no": mark do_not_contact=1 immediately, confirm once
  politely, never message again.
