# Follow-up sequence templates (steps 1-3)

Shared rules: each follow-up must ADD something new — never "just bumping
this". Same channel as step 0. Stop instantly on any reply (the
response-classifier owns what happens next).

## Step 1 — Day 3 (<= 80 words)
Angle: a second, different proof point or a concrete example of the outcome.
Shape: "One thing I didn't mention — <new proof/example>. <Restated
one-line ask>." Reference the first email in half a sentence, don't resend it.

## Step 2 — Day 7 (<= 60 words)
Angle: reduce the ask. Offer the smallest possible next step: a 1-pager, a
2-minute video, a single relevant case study. "No call needed — happy to just
send it."

## Step 3 — Day 14, breakup (<= 50 words)
Angle: polite close. "I'll assume the timing's off and stop here. If <pain>
comes back on the radar, this inbox reaches me." No guilt-tripping, no "did
my emails reach you?". After step 3: lead stage -> `lost` (reason:
no-response) unless the user says otherwise.
