# Cold email — first touch (step 0)

Subject: <= 6 words, lowercase feels human, no clickbait, no "quick question".
Good shapes: "<pain> at <company>?", "<their service> + <our capability>".

Body structure (<= 120 words total):

1. **Opener (1 sentence)** — the personalized observation from
   pain_hypothesis. Specific and true, about THEM, not us.
2. **Bridge (1-2 sentences)** — the pain that observation implies, stated in
   their vocabulary.
3. **Credibility (1 sentence)** — ONE proof point from product.md. Named and
   concrete beats broad and impressive.
4. **Ask (1 sentence)** — low-friction, interest-based:
   "Worth a 15-minute look?" / "Open to seeing how X handles this?"
   Never "book a slot on my calendar" in a first touch.
5. **Opt-out (1 line)** — "If this isn't relevant, reply 'no' and I won't
   write again."
6. **Signature** — sender identity block from product.md, verbatim.

Rules:
- No attachments, no more than one link, no images.
- No flattery openers ("I love what you're doing at...").
- No fake urgency, no "re:" subjects, no [unsubscribe] pretending to be a
  newsletter — this is a 1:1 email from a real person.
